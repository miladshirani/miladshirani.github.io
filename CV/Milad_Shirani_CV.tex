\section{Profile}

Scientific AI researcher and ML engineer with 10+ years combining continuum
mechanics, numerical simulation, differentiable programming, and machine learning
for nonlinear solids and coupled multiphysics. Develops physics-constrained neural
networks, neural operators, and differentiable scientific-computing systems that
embed governing equations, variational principles, conservation laws, and
thermodynamic consistency directly into computation. Built data-free GNN solvers
that minimize FEM weak-form residuals, matrix-free differentiable finite-element
solvers using autodiff and Newton--Krylov methods, and differentiable meshless
peridynamics for fracture and delamination. Demonstrated an 11.6$\times$ GPU
tangent-product speedup for differentiable FEA, 8--10$\times$ speedup for meshless mechanics,
and 35--250$\times$ lower neural-operator query cost than benchmark FEM, with
0.05--3\% agreement against converged finite-element solutions across coupled
systems. Open-source developer with startup experience translating scientific ML
methods into engineering systems.

\item \textbf{Matrix-free differentiable FEA --- Newton--Krylov:}
Built an
\href{https://github.com/miladshirani/Differentiable-FEA}
{open-source}
PyTorch finite-element solver for large-strain hyperelasticity that eliminates

computed through automatic differentiation; each Newton step is solved with

and an $11.6\times$ GPU speedup for tangent products, solving 10,000
elements in 2.9~s.
\href{https://miladshirani.github.io/research-differentiable-fea.html}

% ── Differentiable meshless mechanics ─────────────────────────────────────────

\item \textbf{Differentiable meshless mechanics --- fracture and delamination:}
Developed an autodiff-based meshless peridynamics solver for nonlinear solids,
including brittle fracture, pressure-driven hydraulic fracture, and Mode-I /
mixed-mode delamination without remeshing. Achieved $8$--$10\times$ runtime
speedup and $9.7\times10^{-11}$ agreement with a validated reference solution.
Engineered a matrix-free dynamic-relaxation scheme to overcome Newton--Raphson
tangent singularities and converge through unstable crack propagation.
\href{https://miladshirani.github.io/research-peridynamic-fracture.html}
{Project page}.

% ── Physics-constrained GNNs ──────────────────────────────────────────────────

\item \textbf{Data-free physics-constrained GNNs for multiphysics:}
Developed geometry-informed graph neural networks that solve coupled PDE
systems as implicit time-steppers by minimizing the FEM weak-form residual
directly, without labeled solution data. Embedded conservation laws and
thermodynamic consistency in the computational formulation. The graph shares
the FEM discretization, shape functions, quadrature, and boundary conditions,
making the weak-form residual both the training objective and an intrinsic
physics-based error monitor. Implemented in JAX and PyTorch with residual
parity to $\sim10^{-12}$.

\begin{itemize}[
  leftmargin=1.2em,
  itemsep=1pt,
  topsep=2pt,
  parsep=0pt,
  label=\textcolor{midgray}{\textendash}
]

\item \textbf{PI-GCN --- chemoelasticity:}
Developed a graph-convolutional network for transient finite-strain
chemoelasticity with four coupled fields: displacement, pressure, chemical
potential, and concentration. Used block-weighted weak-form residuals and
Adam $\rightarrow$ L-BFGS optimization with FEM and meshless NEM back ends.
Achieved $0.24\%$ displacement and $0.73\%$ concentration error over 50
implicit time steps.

\item \textbf{PI-EPD-GNN --- thermoelasticity:}
Developed a MeshGraphNets-style Encode--Process--Decode model for transient
heat conduction coupled to finite-deformation elasticity. Used Taylor--Hood
elements, a Voronoi message-passing graph, and tangent-based loss
preconditioning. Achieved $0.14\%$ displacement and $0.011\%$ temperature
error.

\end{itemize}

% ── Neural operators ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

\item \textbf{Physics-constrained neural operators and variational networks:}
Designed label-free energy-minimizing PINNs and DeepONets in PyTorch and JAX
for liquid crystal elastomers, coupling finite-strain deformation and director
fields. Trained over FEM/NEM discretizations using total potential energy,
Gauss quadrature, and exactly enforced boundary conditions. A single operator
predicted full fields in $\sim0.5$--$0.7$ ms, with $35$--$250\times$ lower
query cost than warm FEM on a 156-node benchmark. Achieved $0.7\%$ shear and
$1.6$--$3\%$ uniaxial displacement error on unseen stretches. Validated
quasiconvexity, rank-one convexity, and Legendre--Hadamard conditions.
\href{https://miladshirani.github.io/research-physics-enforced-operator-learning.html}
{Project page}.

% ── Inverse constitutive modeling ─────────────────────────────────────────────

\item \textbf{Inverse constitutive modeling:}
Developed PINN-based inverse frameworks in JAX and PyTorch for learning
polyconvex stored-energy functions for isotropic and anisotropic materials,
including fibrous composites and biological tissues, directly from FEA-generated
datasets, with polyconvexity enforced as a hard architectural constraint.

% ── PI-FNO ────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

\item \textbf{PI-FNO --- open-source Physics-Informed Fourier Neural Operator:}
Designed, implemented, and open-sourced
\href{https://github.com/miladshirani/PI-FNO}{PI-FNO}
for 5-class valvular heart-disease classification from phonocardiograms.
Embedded cardiac periodicity and S1/S2 frequency hierarchy as hard architectural
priors and used a learnable frequency mask initialized from cardiac acoustics.
Achieved 97.5\% test accuracy across 1,000 recordings.
\href{https://miladshirani.github.io/research-pi-fno.html}
{Project page}.

% ── Multiphysics infrastructure ───────────────────────────────────────────────

\item \textbf{Multiphysics simulation infrastructure:}
Built FEM and multiphysics frameworks using FEniCSx, COMSOL, JAX, and PyTorch
for gel swelling, thermoelasticity, and fibrous biomaterials, providing
high-fidelity references for surrogate training and benchmarking. Currently
extending the hydrogel swelling model to biological tissues and to delamination
of layered hydrogel systems.
